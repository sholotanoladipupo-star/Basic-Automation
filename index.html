Our Story

Born in Lagos.
Worn Everywhere.

Lagos Luxe was founded in 2019 by a collective of passionate Nigerian designers who believed that African fashion deserved a global platform. We blend Afro-contemporary aesthetics with precision tailoring to create pieces that are unapologetically bold, timeless, and unmistakably Nigerian.

Every fabric is ethically sourced. Every stitch is intentional. From Ankara prints to premium linen, we celebrate the full spectrum of Nigerian style.
